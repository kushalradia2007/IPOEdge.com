# IPOEdge.com

## 📈 Overview
IPOEdge is a comprehensive, all-in-one platform designed to provide deep insights, historical data tracking, and interactive learning resources for Initial Public Offerings (IPOs) in the Indian stock market. Whether you are a beginner looking to understand market fundamentals or an analyst evaluating listing gains, IPOEdge serves as your centralized hub for financial discovery.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Extensive Historical Database:** Access meticulously detailed data on all Indian IPOs from the past 15 years. Analyze critical metrics for every listing, including the original issue price, listing price, listing gains/losses, and current market price.
- **Interactive AI Assistant:** An integrated, intelligent chatbot is available 24/7 to clear doubts, explain complex financial terminology, and provide immediate answers to your market-related questions.
- **Educational Video Hub:** A dedicated learning section featuring curated video content to help users understand exactly what an IPO is, how the bidding process works, and how to evaluate potential investments.
- **Centralized Archive:** Source code and assets are securely packaged for easy deployment and local testing.
